{# ---- Methodology --------------------------------------------------------- #}

How the score works

  1. Metric scores. For each measurement, the best city gets 100 and the worst gets 0; others are placed proportionally in between. For things like crime or rent, lower values score higher.
  2. Category scores. Each category is the average of its metric scores.
  3. Overall score. A weighted average of category scores using your priorities. If a city has no data for a category, the other categories are re-weighted for that city.
